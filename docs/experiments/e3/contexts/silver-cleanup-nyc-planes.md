This source is the planes table of the nycflights13 R data package: the FAA aircraft registry's 2014 release, reduced to the tail numbers found among the 2013 New York City flights; American Airlines and Envoy Air reported fleet numbers rather than tail numbers, so their aircraft do not appear. As the package documents the columns: tailnum is the tail number; year is the year manufactured; type is the type of plane; manufacturer and model are as the registry records them; engines and seats are counts; speed is the average cruising speed in mph; engine is the type of engine. The publisher writes NA where a value is missing. We name a measure with its unit as a suffix (minutes, miles, mph, inches, degrees, feet, percent). We name a code column with `_code` and a boolean with `is_`. We carry a calendar date as one DATE column rather than three integer parts. We spell out abbreviated column names.
