This source is the flights table of the nycflights13 R data package: every 2013 departure from the three New York City airports (JFK, LGA, EWR) as the Bureau of Transportation Statistics reported it, here the January to June window. As the package documents the columns: year, month, and day are the date of departure; dep_time and arr_time are the actual departure and arrival times in HHMM or HMM form, local time, and sched_dep_time and sched_arr_time are the scheduled ones; dep_delay and arr_delay are in minutes, negative when early; carrier is the two-letter carrier abbreviation; flight is the flight number; tailnum is the plane's tail number; origin and dest are airport codes; air_time is minutes in the air; distance is miles between the airports; hour and minute are the scheduled departure time split into its parts; time_hour is the scheduled date and hour of the flight, here as a UTC instant. A flight that never departed has no departure time. We name a measure with its unit as a suffix (minutes, miles, mph, inches, degrees, feet, percent). We name a code column with `_code` and a boolean with `is_`. We carry a calendar date as one DATE column rather than three integer parts. We spell out abbreviated column names.
