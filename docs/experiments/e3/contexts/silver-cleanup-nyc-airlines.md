This source is the airlines table of the nycflights13 R data package: the Bureau of Transportation Statistics carrier lookup, reduced to the sixteen carriers that flew from New York City in 2013. As the package documents the columns: carrier is the two-letter abbreviation, and name is the carrier's full name. We name a measure with its unit as a suffix (minutes, miles, mph, inches, degrees, feet, percent). We name a code column with `_code` and a boolean with `is_`. We carry a calendar date as one DATE column rather than three integer parts. We spell out abbreviated column names.
