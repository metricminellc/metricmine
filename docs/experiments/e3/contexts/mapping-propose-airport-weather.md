This source is the unified airport weather table of the silver layer: the nycflights13 hourly observations at the three New York City airports joined to the OurAirports airports and runways, with the airport join's completeness measured and declared in the table's contract (complete). Temperatures are degrees Fahrenheit, wind is mph and degrees, precipitation is inches, pressure is millibars, and visibility is miles. We keep every field name as the silver contract names it.
